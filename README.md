# 📘 Email Cleaner

A lightweight Python script for automatically cleaning, organizing, and maintaining your email inbox. The tool connects to your mailbox via IMAP, scans folders, applies filters, and processes emails based on your custom rules. No external configuration files or dependencies required — everything runs inside a single `main.py`.

---

## 🧩 Overview

The Email Cleaner helps you keep your inbox organized by automatically filtering, sorting, or deleting emails based on your preferences. It is designed for users who want a simple, script‑based solution without complex setup, external services, or additional files.

---

## ⭐ Features

- Connects to your email account via IMAP  
- Reads and processes emails from selected folders  
- Supports whitelist, blacklist, and subject filtersp  
- Safe mode for testing without deleting anything  
- Works with Web.de, GMX, Gmail (IMAP), and other providers  
- Single‑file architecture (`main.py` only)
